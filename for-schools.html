For schools

Bring YCDI into your school.

We support students in the areas exams don't measure. Character, faith, confidence and a sense of direction. On your terms, and under safeguarding you can inspect first.

What a partnership looks like

We can run regular fellowships, deliver teaching and character sessions, mentor students, and support your young people through leadership programmes and, where it fits, academic help. What we do is shaped around what your school actually needs. We fit around you, not the other way round.

We've done this in schools across six states, and we do it in a way that respects your authority, your timetable, and your duty of care.

What you can ask us for

  • Weekly or termly student fellowships
  • Character and life-skills sessions
  • One-to-one and small-group mentoring
  • Leadership training for prefects and fellowship leaders
  • Academic support and scholarship referrals
Safeguarding

How we keep your students safe.

This is the part that matters most, so we won't be vague about it. Every YCDI volunteer who works with students is screened before they're allowed near a school. Our people work in twos, never alone with a student in a private space. They don't contact students privately through personal phones or social media. They follow a written code of conduct, and any concern is reported and acted on straight away.

You keep full oversight. We operate on your premises under your rules, and we're glad to share our safeguarding policy, our volunteers' details and our references before anything begins. Read our full safeguarding commitment.
