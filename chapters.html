Our chapters

Find YCDI near you.

YCDI runs as local chapters, each one a team of volunteers going into schools, running fellowships and walking with students. Here is where we are, and a little of how each chapter began.

Benin

Where it all started.

Edo State · Since 2017

Coordinator: Djorba George, with a team of volunteers

Some of our schools: Carrick Memorial, Eghosa Grammar School, Idia College

YCDI began in Benin in 2017 at the University of Benin, Ekehuan campus. It was passion meeting opportunity. Dr. Egbonim had come to Nigeria looking for like-minded people to reach young people, and found Jegede Joseph, who took the idea to his coursemates Christopher Olatunji, Olatunde Godfrey, Ayaneche Cornelius and Obasi Cyril.

The team started visiting schools around the campus. Years on, the work still thrives here under Djorba George and a committed group of volunteers.

Auchi

Steady, patient growth.

Edo State · Since 2017

Coordinators: Brother Evans and Sister Faith

Some of our schools: Rising Star (Jattu), St. Joseph

YCDI was introduced to Auchi in 2017 by Brother Chris Olatunji and Joseph from Lagos. The formal work took shape in 2018 with the first regional conference and a partnership with the St. Joseph's school fellowship.

When Sister Faith joined that year, the chapter found its feet. By 2019 the team was opening its own schools, and it grew to nine. In June 2021, Auchi hosted its own regional conference, a real milestone for a chapter that started small and kept going.

Ilesa, Osun

A young chapter, already reaching far.

Osun State · Since 2022

Coordinator: Pastor Israel Abiodun

Volunteers: Sister Grace Sunday, Brother Hosanna Adebayo, Sister Esther Babatunde

Some of our schools: Miracle Group of Schools, Babalola Memorial Girls' Grammar School, First Class Secondary School, Hope Grammar School

YCDI Osun began in Ilesa in 2022, when Pastor Israel Abiodun took on the coordination and shifted his focus to the mission. Since then the chapter has reached many students, some of whom have carried what they learned into new towns after graduating.

The team works to strengthen students academically, morally and spiritually through school fellowships and mentoring, and the Ilesa chapter continues to flourish.

Ogbomosho

Our newest ground.

Oyo State

Coordinator: Brother Samuel Falaye

Ogbomosho is our newest chapter, an expansion out of the work in Ilesa. Brother Samuel Falaye leads it, carrying the mission into Oyo State and reaching a new set of young lives.

Irele, Ondo

The vision, taken home.

Ondo State · Since 2019

Coordinator: Abisere Caleb

Some of our schools: United Grammar School (Ode-Irele), Arerin Grammar School, Diamond College, Premier College

YCDI Ondo began in Irele in 2019, started by Brother Olatunde Godfrey, who after graduating in Benin saw the need to bring the same work to his hometown. He shared the vision with people who believed in it, and the team began regular school visits.

Three conferences have been held so far, with many lives touched. The chapter is now led by Abisere Caleb.

Agbor

Fast growth, real reach.

Delta State · Since February 2022

Coordinator: Brother Andrew Onwuzurike, with a team of pastors and volunteers

Some of our schools: Igumbor-Otiku, Dein Palace, Ime-Obi, Emuhu Secondary

Agbor was established in February 2022. In just two years the chapter expanded to more than 20 schools and reached over 5,000 teenagers, supported by a strong team of volunteers.

Alongside school fellowships, Agbor has run conferences drawing more than 400 students from across the region, strengthening the work both locally and nationally.

Lagos

Just getting started.

Lagos State · Since 2024

First school: Unity Grammar School, Oshodi

YCDI came to Lagos in 2024 and began school visits straight after receiving approval from the Lagos Education District VI, starting at Unity Grammar School in Oshodi. It is early days, but the ground is being laid.
