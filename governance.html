Trust & safety  /  Governance

How YCDI is led, and held to account.

We ask people to trust us with their children, their time and their money. That trust has to be earned.

The people who lead

YCDI is governed by a Board of Trustees, the body ultimately responsible for our governance, strategy and finances. Day to day, the National Coordinator leads a national team, and each chapter is led by a Regional Coordinator drawn from the local community.

How accountability works

Authority in YCDI is given to serve, not to rule. The lines are clear and they run all the way up. Chapter volunteers report to their Regional Coordinator. Regional Coordinators report to the National Coordinator. The National Coordinator reports to the Board of Trustees. The Board answers to the members and, in the end, to God and the mission.

The checks we keep

Our leaders work to written performance agreements and report on a fixed schedule, monthly at chapter level and quarterly and annually up the chain. Trustees disclose conflicts of interest every year and step back from any decision where they have one. No trustee, officer or volunteer takes private benefit from YCDI's funds or assets beyond approved, documented expenses. Our governing documents are reviewed on a regular cycle so they don't go stale.

Raising a concern

If you have a concern about how YCDI is led, how our money is handled, or how we govern ourselves, you can raise it, and you'll be protected for doing so in good faith. Concerns can go to the National Coordinator, to the Board Chair, or, if you'd rather stay anonymous, in writing to the Board Secretary. Safeguarding concerns follow the separate route on our safeguarding page. We take every genuine concern seriously and treat it in confidence.
