Volunteer

If you love young people and have time to give, we'd be glad to have you. Volunteers teach, mentor, plan events, create content and lead chapters. Be the mentor you wish you'd had.

Because you'd be working with young people, everyone goes through the same careful process before serving. An application, a short conversation, two Christian character references, a safeguarding declaration, and orientation. It's thorough on purpose. Protecting the young people in our care comes before everything.
