Who we are

A Christian NGO that stays long enough to change a life.

We're run mostly by volunteers who give their time to young people. We started in 2017, registered a year later, and grew from one town to eight chapters across six states. Thousands of students have come through our fellowships, conferences and mentoring since.

Our conviction is simple. Every young person is made for a purpose. Faith gives them the root, and the right people help them grow into it. So we don't preach once and leave. We stay, we disciple, and we open doors a student couldn't open alone.

What we do

Everything points to one outcome.

A young person who knows Christ, grows in character, and goes on to lead others. Here's how that happens on the ground.

School outreach

We go where the students are, running fellowships and teaching inside secondary and tertiary schools. Always with the school's permission, always to the safeguarding standards we hold ourselves to. For most students, this is where they first meet YCDI.

Discipleship & mentorship

Small groups and one-to-one mentoring that carry a student for years, not a single visit. Always open, never private.

Conferences & leadership

Retreats and youth conferences that stretch students and put a bigger vision in front of them.

Scholarships & academic support

Tutoring and scholarship help for students with the ability but not the means to stay in school.

Digital ministry

Teaching and encouragement online, so the discipleship keeps going between meetings.

Rooted and Rising

From their first hello to leading others.

Our discipleship isn't random. It moves a young person through five stages, and nobody is meant to stay a spectator.

Stage 1

Contact

The first meeting. A school visit, a conference, a friend who brought them along.

Stage 2

Connect

They join a fellowship or small group and start to belong.

Stage 3

Commit

They make a real decision to follow Christ.

Stage 4

Grow

They're discipled, mentored and given responsibility.

Stage 5

Multiply

They begin leading and discipling others. The cycle starts again.

Built on trust

We ask you to trust us with your children and your giving.

So we're open about how we keep young people safe, how we're governed, and how we handle every naira.

Safeguarding

Screening for everyone who works with children, the two-adult rule, no private contact, and a clear way to report a concern.

Governance

A Board of Trustees, accountability that runs all the way up, and a confidential channel to raise concerns.

Financial accountability
