What we do

Everything points to one outcome.

A young person who knows Christ, grows in character, and goes on to lead others. Here's how that happens on the ground.

School outreach

We go where the students are. Our volunteers run fellowships, teaching and honest conversations about faith, character and the road ahead, inside secondary and tertiary schools. Always with the school's permission, always to the standards on our safeguarding page. For most students, this is the first time they meet YCDI.

Discipleship and mentorship

A single meeting rarely changes a life. Depth does. We disciple students in small groups and mentor them one to one, walking with them over months and years rather than dropping in once and moving on. Mentoring happens in open, accountable settings, never in private or hidden ones.

Conferences and leadership development

Every year we gather young people for retreats, workshops and conferences that put a bigger vision in front of them and hand them the tools to lead. These are the moments students remember years later.

Scholarships and academic support

Faith and character don't cancel out the need for a good education. We run tutoring and study groups, and we help students with real ability but no money to stay in school through scholarship support.

Digital ministry

Not every young person is in a room we can reach. We teach and encourage through social media and online content, so the discipleship carries on between meetings.

Volunteer development

None of this happens without people. We recruit, screen, train and deploy volunteers, and we keep investing in them so they serve well and grow while they do. Here's how to join them.

Rooted and Rising

Our discipleship pathway, in five stages.

It moves a young person from the first hello to the day they're discipling someone else. Nobody is meant to stay a spectator.

1

Contact

The first meeting. A school visit, a conference, a friend who brought them along.

2

Connect

They join a fellowship or small group and start to belong.

3

Commit

They make a real decision to follow Christ.

4

Grow

They're discipled, mentored and given responsibility.

5

Multiply

They begin leading and discipling others. The cycle starts again.
