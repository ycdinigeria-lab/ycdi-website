Find a chapter

We run in Benin, Auchi, Agbor, Ibadan, Ogbomoso, Osun (Ilesha), Irele and Lagos. For the safety of the students we work with, chapter enquiries are handled through the central office rather than personal numbers. Tell us which chapter you mean and we'll connect you.

Raising a safeguarding concern

Safeguarding concerns are handled separately and with care. See how to report a concern. If a child is in immediate danger, contact the police on 112 or 199, or NAPTIP on 0800 800 8000.
