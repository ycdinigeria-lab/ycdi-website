Trust & safety  /  Financial accountability

Where the money comes from, and where it goes.

How we manage money is part of our witness, not separate from it. So we're plain about it.

Our income

YCDI is funded by gifts from individuals, churches and organisations, by grants, by fundraising, and by occasional programme income where the Board approves it. Every source is lawful, recorded and accounted for.

How we handle it

Money is not left to one person's discretion. Our funds sit in proper organisational accounts that require two signatories, not one. Spending above set limits needs written approval, and larger amounts need the Board. Our Financial Secretary keeps complete records to recognised accounting standards. Restricted gifts and grants are tracked separately and spent only on what they were given for.

The controls, in short

  • Dual signatories on every account
  • Written approval thresholds for spending
  • Restricted gifts tracked and ring-fenced
  • External audit within 90 days of year-end
  • Audited statements presented to members
How we're audited

Our accounts are audited every year by an independent, qualified external auditor appointed by the Board. The audited statements are completed within ninety days of the year-end and presented to our members. Where the audit flags anything to improve, we act on it and report back.

Staying compliant

We meet our obligations to the Corporate Affairs Commission, the Federal Inland Revenue Service and the anti-money-laundering authorities, and we keep to donor conditions on every grant we accept. Compliance isn't paperwork for its own sake. It's how we stay trustworthy.

If you give

You can give knowing your gift will be recorded, used for the purpose you intended, and accounted for. If you fund a specific programme or give a larger amount, we're glad to report back to you on what it achieved.
