About

We stay long enough to change a life.

A Christian NGO, run mostly by volunteers, helping young Nigerians grow in faith, character and purpose since 2017.

Who we are

The Young Christian Development Initiative is a faith-based Nigerian NGO. We exist to help young people become who God made them to be, spiritually, academically, and in the way they carry themselves through life.

Most of our work is done by people who aren't paid for it. Teachers, graduates, professionals and ministers give their time because they've seen what happens when a young person is discipled well and mentored properly.

How we started

We began in 2017 as the Christian Youth Development Programme, a small effort to reach students with more than a one-off message. In 2018 we registered under our current name and kept growing. What was one location is now eight chapters across six states, and the model has stayed the same throughout. Go into schools, build real relationships, disciple for the long haul, and help young people rise.

YCDI at a glance

  • Founded 2017, registered as YCDI in 2018
  • Eight chapters across six states
  • More than 5,000 students reached
  • Volunteer-driven, faith-anchored, KJV throughout our ministry
  • Registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission of Nigeria
Vision & mission
Our vision

To raise a generation of young leaders empowered by godliness and committed to impacting society positively.

Our mission

To inspire, train, and equip youth with the knowledge, skills, and spiritual principles needed to succeed in life and make meaningful contributions to their communities.

What we value

Five things we won't bend on.

Faith and spiritual growth

A living relationship with God is the root of everything else, so every programme points young people back to it.

Integrity

We hold to honesty and moral uprightness, and we don't bend biblical standards for convenience or gain.

Leadership and excellence

Every young person is made for a purpose and capable of leading. We nurture that with vision, character and skill.

Community impact

We're blessed to be a blessing. We push our students and volunteers to serve and leave something lasting behind.

Inclusion and collaboration

We welcome young people and partners across ethnic and denominational Christian backgrounds, and we work with churches and schools to reach further.

What we believe

YCDI stands on the historic Christian faith. One God revealed as Father, Son and Holy Spirit. The Bible as the inspired Word of God. Salvation by grace through faith in Jesus Christ. And the call in the Great Commission to reach and disciple young people. We hold these firmly while making room for the honest differences that exist between orthodox Christian traditions.

Who leads YCDI

YCDI is led by a Board of Trustees, a Founder and International Coordinator, and a National Coordinator, supported by a national team and Regional Coordinators in each chapter.
