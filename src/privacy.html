Legal  /  Privacy

Privacy policy.

Any information you share with YCDI is received in trust. This explains what we collect, why, and the say you have over it.

Last updated September 2026 · Reference YCDI-LEG-003 · In line with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR)

The short version

We collect only what we need, keep it secure, and never sell it. You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold. For anyone under 18, we ask a parent or guardian first. Everything below is the detail behind that.

Who we are

YCDI (the Young Christian Development Initiative) is a registered Nigerian non-governmental organisation, CAC/IT/NO 127209. Our National Coordinator acts as the data controller for the information we hold. If you have any question about your data, write to info@ycdinigeria.org.

What we collect, and why

When you fill in a form on this site, to volunteer, partner, share a story or get in touch, we collect your name, your email, and whatever else you choose to tell us, such as your phone number, location or message. We use it only to reply and to do what you asked us to do.

When you give through Paystack, your payment is handled by Paystack directly. We never see your card details. We receive a record of the gift so we can thank you and account for it properly.

When you simply browse, the service that hosts the site may log basic technical data such as an IP address, in aggregate, to keep the site running and secure. We do not use advertising trackers.

In our wider work we also hold information on students, volunteers, donors and partners. That is governed by our full Data Protection and Privacy Policy, which we are glad to share on request.

Children and young people

Most of what we do is with people under 18, so we take this seriously. We ask for parental or guardian consent before collecting a young person's personal information, and we never publish a child's photo, name or story without documented consent from them and their parent or guardian.

The basis we rely on

Depending on the situation, we process data on the basis of your consent (parental consent for minors), our legitimate interest in running the ministry, or a contract with you. Where consent is the basis, it is freely given, specific, informed and recorded, and you can withdraw it at any time without penalty.

How we keep it safe

Personal data is kept on password-protected systems, with access limited to the people who genuinely need it. We do not store it on personal drives or send it to personal email accounts, and we keep it only for as long as it is needed before secure disposal.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data or share it for marketing. We rely on a few trusted services to run the site, chiefly Netlify, which hosts the site and handles form submissions, and Paystack, which processes donations. Beyond that, we share information only with your consent, or where the law or a safeguarding duty requires it.

Your rights

Under the NDPR you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or provide it in a portable format. We respond within 30 days. You can also withdraw consent at any time, which takes effect immediately. To exercise any of these, email info@ycdinigeria.org for the attention of the National Coordinator.

If something goes wrong

If personal data is ever lost or exposed, we assess it quickly, and where there is a high risk to anyone affected we notify the National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA) within 72 hours and tell those affected without undue delay.

Changes to this notice

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top always shows the current version.
